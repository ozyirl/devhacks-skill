# Commute Cram

A [Cubbie](https://cubbie.ai) Skill that turns your notes, slides or Canvas materials into a podcast-style MP3 you
can review on the bus, light rail or walk to class: a host explains, a second voice quizzes you, a chime gives you
time to answer out loud, then you hear the answer. Sized to your commute.

Install: text this folder's link to Cubbie and reply "yes".

- `SKILL.md`: what Cubbie does, step by step, and the one-time Setup
- `scripts/cram.py`: makes the MP3 with offline Piper voices and ffmpeg (try `--sample`)
- `references/script-guide.md`: how to write a script that works by ear

Voices: [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) `en_US-lessac-medium` and `en_US-ryan-medium`.
