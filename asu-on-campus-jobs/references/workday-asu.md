## Search

- Type keywords or a JR number in Workday's own search on that page (left-side Find Student Jobs box). Browser-page find can miss titles that are not in the visible chunk.
- Wait until Job Profile and campus facets are on screen before clicking filters.
- Job Family: Student Employee.
- Campus: Tempe checkbox when the student asked for Tempe. Workday facets often intercept a normal click; click the input via the page script if a regular click does nothing.
- Direct posting URLs like `https://www.myworkday.com/asu/d/inst/15$158872/{id}.htmld` often hang with a spinner if opened in a new tab. Close that tab, go back to Find Student Jobs, search the JR, then open the row.

## Pay

Many list rows hide the dollar amount. Prefer pay printed on the posting. If the posting only names a student worker scale, use this ASU hourly band as a hint (it can change; posting text wins):

- Worker I: about $14.70–$15.40
- Worker II: about $15.10–$16.57
- Worker III: about $15.97–$18.97
- Worker IV: about $17.75 and up

A student who asked for $16–17 usually fits Worker III, and Worker II only if the posting's top of band reaches $16.

## Funding / F-1

The funding field is the Work-Study filter. "Federal Work Study" or "70/30% ASU" is FWS. "No Federal Funding" is the usual non-FWS hourly job. International students who asked to skip FWS should not be shown FWS-only rows.

## Apply

- Resume upload on the first Quick Apply screen is optional and used for auto-fill. Formatted PDFs often parse badly there. Skip that box.
- Attach the student's resume PDF on the later Resume/CV step. Files cannot be added or swapped after Submit.
- Some tech roles (Software Aide and similar) require a cover letter. Draft, show, wait, then attach.
- Cap during a fall/spring term is typically 20 hours/week; mention it only if the posting or the student brings hours up.

## Tech-ish titles (examples, not a closed list)

Information Systems Grader, Software Aide, System Technician, CIS research aide, SCAI grader or IA, Engineering tutor, laboratory or classroom tech when the posting is clearly computing.
