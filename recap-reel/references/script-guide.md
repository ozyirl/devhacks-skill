# Writing a Recap Reel script

A recap reel is watched on a phone, often with the sound on and attention short. The narration carries the
learning; the screen shows the few words worth remembering.

## Length

- About 150 spoken words per minute. A 60-second reel is about 140 to 160 words of narration in total.
- `intro`: one short sentence, a hook ("Cells in sixty seconds. Here's what's on the exam.").
- 3 to 6 `scenes`, 20 to 35 words of narration each. At most 8.
- `outro`: one short sentence ("Save this for exam night.").

## Each scene

- `heading`: 1 to 3 words, the idea's name ("Mitochondria", "Supply shift").
- `points`: up to 3, each 2 to 5 words; they appear one by one as the narration goes, so list them in the order
  the narration says them.
- `narration`: spoken sentences that explain the points. Short sentences, one idea each.

## Rules

- **Only from their material.** Use the student's slides, notes or course files; don't add facts that aren't
  there.
- **Say symbols out loud** in the narration ("H two O", "x squared"), but keep the symbol in points ("H₂O").
- **No URLs, tables or "as you can see".** The screen only shows the heading, points and captions.
- **Hook first.** The first two seconds decide whether people keep watching.
- `course` is optional; it shows as a small tag at the top (for example "BIO 181").
