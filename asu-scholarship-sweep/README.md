# ASU Scholarship Sweep

A [Cubbie](https://cubbie.ai) Skill that helps ASU students find and win scholarships. Cubbie signs in to ASU's
Scholarship Universe with you, finds what you qualify for, tracks every deadline and dollar, helps you write essays
from your own stories, sends calendar reminders, and submits only after you say yes.

Install: text this folder's link to Cubbie and reply "yes".

- `SKILL.md`: what Cubbie does, step by step
- `scripts/tracker.py`: deadlines, amounts, status and calendar reminders (stdlib only; try `list --sample`)
- `references/essays.md`: essay rules and scam signs
