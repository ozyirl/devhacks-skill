# ASU Lab Match

A [Cubbie](https://cubbie.ai) Skill that helps ASU undergrads get into research. Text your interests and resume;
Cubbie finds professors whose research fits in ASU's public faculty directory, explains why, drafts a short
personal email, sends it only after you say yes, and reminds you to follow up.

Install: text this folder's link to Cubbie and reply "yes".

- `SKILL.md`: what Cubbie does, step by step
- `scripts/find_labs.py`: searches and ranks faculty, tracks who you emailed (stdlib only; try `search --sample`)
- `references/first-email.md`: how to write a first email professors answer
